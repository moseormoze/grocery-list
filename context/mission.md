# Mission

> **Placeholder — to be finalized in the first Discovery cycle.**

A shared grocery list two partners can build and maintain together. One list, two phones, always in sync.

## Who It's For
The user and their partner. Built for one household first; multi-household / multi-group scope is out unless Discovery changes that.

## The Friction It Removes
- "Did you already add milk?" — no more duplicate items.
- Lost paper lists, fragmented Notes-app lists, screenshots in chats.
- Forgetting what the other person asked for.
- Re-typing the same staples every week.
