# Goals

> **Placeholder — to be finalized in the first Discovery cycle.**

## MVP Success Criteria (draft)
- [ ] Two users can share one list with near-real-time sync
- [ ] Adding an item takes under 5 seconds end-to-end
- [ ] Hebrew UI, RTL-correct on mobile browsers
- [ ] Works as an installable PWA on iOS and Android
- [ ] Total monthly infra cost under $5

## Non-Goals (draft)
- No native mobile apps in MVP
- No price tracking, store integrations, or coupons
- No meal planning / recipes
- No more than two people per list in MVP (unless Discovery says otherwise)

## Success Signals (post-MVP)
- The couple reaches for the app instead of a Notes / WhatsApp message
- Both partners contribute to the list, not just one
- A week passes without anyone reverting to paper
